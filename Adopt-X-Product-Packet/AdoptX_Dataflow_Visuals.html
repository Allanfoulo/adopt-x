Adopt X Dataflow Visuals

Static visual companion for the approved MVP workflow.

1. Intake

Source Hits

Structured public sources are captured with provenance and scan-run linkage.

2. Normalize

Deal Candidates

Raw hits are deduped and converted into canonical working records.

3. Review

Triage Queue

Analyst approves, rejects, and edits tags or scores with audit history.

4. Output

Brief Archive

Approved candidates become structured adoption briefs and dashboard entries.

Intake -> Normalize -> Review -> Brief
Control rule: social or community research can enrich a candidate, but cannot bypass the candidate queue in the MVP.
